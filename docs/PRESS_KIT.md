# Frontier: press kit

**Play:** https://g8r-b8.github.io/frontierLab/ · **Source:** https://github.com/g8r-b8/frontierLab

## One-liner
Found a frontier AI lab. Race to AGI. Try not to repeat history.

## Short description (≈50 words)
Frontier is a Democracy-style strategy game about running an AI lab. You set policies on sliders and
watch the effects ripple through a living web of compute, talent, safety, hype and regulators. Then
you find out whether you'll build aligned AGI, lose the race, run out of money, or get fired by your own board.

## Long description
You're the founder of a frontier AI lab, and you have ten years.

- **A living cause-and-effect web.** Forty-four policies, sixteen stats and ten stakeholder groups,
  all wired together with delays, in the style of Positech's *Democracy*. Hover anything to see
  what drives it.
- **History rhymes.** Eighteen pitfalls drawn from real AI and startup history build up quietly
  and show warning signs before they spring: the board coup, the copyright reckoning, the safety
  exodus, the efficiency shock, the blog post.
- **Real quotes everywhere.** More than 170 verified quotes, memos and headlines appear on the
  loading screen, in the quarterly newspaper and in a collectible archive. Triumphs and disasters both.
- **Hardware that ages.** Chip generations, build delays, failures, depreciation, power limits,
  and compute deals with strings attached.
- **People and culture.** Hire individuals from a LinkedIn-style feed, appoint hiring managers,
  pick hiring practices and company values, including Uber's original fourteen, and live with
  the consequences.
- **Marketing that matters.** Brand versus hype, Super Bowl ads (Q1 only), waitlists, referral
  loops, and a decision on every launch day.
- **Multiplayer, peer to peer.** Real-time races against friends' labs with no game server.
- **Play with Claude.** A terminal bridge lets you, or Claude Code acting as your chief of staff,
  read and drive the live game.

## Facts
- Platform: web browser (desktop recommended). Free and open source.
- Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no tracking.
- Sound: all synthesized live with WebAudio.

## A note on real people
Real people and companies appear only in sourced historical artifacts and history notes. Every
direct quote is verified, and anything else is marked as a paraphrase. The labs, chips, providers
and candidates you meet in play are fictional.
